1 · Hardware chain

Signal path from detector strip through VMM3a ASIC and FEC FPGA to UDP packet on the network. Key numbers: 64 channels per VMM chip, Spartan7 FPGA at 44.44 MHz (22.5 ns clock), 9 010-byte UDP datagrams on port 6006.

2 · VMM3a hit processing

Inside the VMM3a ASIC: discriminator fires on threshold crossing, BCID is latched, the pulse shaper reaches its peak, the peak finder triggers the TAC, and the ADC digitises the amplitude. Output is a 6-byte hit word containing channel, BCID, TDC, ADC, and offset.

3 · Clock, markers, offset, and TDC

Top panel: overview timeline — SRS markers reset the offset counter every ~92 µs (one 4096-tick BCID cycle). Hit offset + BCID give the coarse time; TDC gives the sub-22.5 ns fine-time correction. Bottom panel: zoom into 5 BC bins (0–112.5 ns) showing the five steps in order. ① Threshold at t = 5 ns latches BCID = 2000. ② Shaping time = 90 ns (4 BC bins, chip ST register, range 25–200 ns, not recorded per-hit) carries the pulse from threshold to peak. ③ Peak found at t = 95 ns in BCID = 2004 — TAC starts. ④ BC edge at t = 112.5 ns — TAC stops; TDC = 75 → 17.5 ns. ⑤ chip_time = (BCID_thr+1)×T_BC − TDC×60/256 ≈ t_threshold; resolution 0.234 ns/count.

4 · Timestamp reconstruction

Combining all fields into an absolute hit time: abs_time_ns = srs_timestamp × 25 ns + (offset × 4096 + BCID) × 22.5 ns − TDC × 60 ns / 256. The srs_timestamp (42-bit FEC free-running counter at 40 MHz) provides the long-range absolute reference; chip_time gives the fine-grained hit position within the ~92 µs BCID cycle.
